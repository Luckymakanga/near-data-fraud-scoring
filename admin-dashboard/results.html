What this page shows

The study compares three ways of scoring transactions for fraud with the same model: an external batch job that polls the database, scoring inside the database as each row is stored, and scoring in the application tier (the design this live demo uses). Every figure below comes from the study's benchmark scripts and result files; none is measured on this dashboard or on the live server.

Decision latency is the time from a transaction’s scheduled arrival to the moment its fraud score is saved, so waiting in a queue counts. The data are a public dataset of European card transactions used as a stand-in; they say nothing about fraud rates in Uganda.
