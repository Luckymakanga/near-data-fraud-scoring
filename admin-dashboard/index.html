About this prototype

What it is

A university research prototype that gives each card transaction a fraud score from 0 to 1 and a decision, and shows how that decision was reached. It is part of a study of where fraud scoring should run in a bank: outside the database, inside it, or in the application.

About the data

The transactions come from a public dataset of European card payments, not from any bank. Fields V1 to V28 are anonymised numbers with no human meaning, so there is no location, branch or merchant. Customer names are demo labels.

What to try

  1. Under “Analyze a Transaction”, click Load Random Transaction, then choose a customer, then Analyze.
  2. Open “How this score was made” to see why it got its decision.
  3. Look up a customer, and filter Recent Transactions by decision.
  4. Upload a CSV, or open Research results at the top.

What the numbers mean

Decision: BLOCK stops the payment, REVIEW sends it to an analyst, MONITOR allows it but watches it, ALLOW has no concern. Flagged means the model judged it fraud. Prevention rate is the share of the money in all transactions that was in flagged ones.
